# Design Context: Orbit

## Users
- Puzzle enthusiasts looking for a focused, "executive toy" experience. The game is played in moments of high focus or meditative downtime.

## Brand Personality
- **Voice**: Minimal, Round, Fast.
- **Tone**: Refined, intentional, and unimpressed by clutter.
- **Emotional Goal**: Calm tactical focus.

## Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: Dark Mode (Immersive and moody).
- **Style**: Refined/Editorial. High-end typography, monochromatic base with sharp precision accents, and spacious visual rhythm.
- **Anti-references**: Avoid generic "gaming" neon or cluttered dashboard cards.

## Design Principles
1. **Precision over Decoration**: Every line and circle should feel like it was drawn by a drafting compass.
2. **Kinetic Stillness**: Animations should be fast and precise (no bounce), emphasizing the "round" nature of the board.
3. **Typographic Authority**: Use a distinctive, high-contrast font pairing to lead the hierarchy.
4. **Void Space**: Use the dark theme to create a sense of infinite depth around the board.
